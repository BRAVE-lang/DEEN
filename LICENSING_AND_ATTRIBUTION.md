## 5. 99 Names of Allah (Asma ul-Husna)

- **Source**: Quranic verses and the authentic Sunnah (compilation from Jami' at-Tirmidhi #3507, Sahih al-Bukhari #2736, and Sunan Ibn Majah #3861).
- **Scholarly Clarification**: The application clearly notes that while Allah has more attributes than can be enumerated, these 99 names are established in the authentic classical scholarly traditions for reflection and invocation.
